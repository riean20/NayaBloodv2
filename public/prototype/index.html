Questions
answered.

Can I mint from this page?+

No. The mint buttons are disabled previews. Contract methods, rules, and wallet signing have not been integrated.

Are these the final NFT artworks?+

The image alt text, captions, and names below identify the supplied artwork cards only. They do not assert on-chain item IDs or a one-to-one mapping to individual tokens.

Are the contract addresses verified?+

They are candidate addresses provided for this prototype. Verify network, ownership, source, ABI, mint availability, price, limits, and other rules before connecting any mint flow.

What is NayaBloodv2?+

This is a first local website prototype for the new NayaBloodv2 site name, based on the dark brown and gold visual direction shared in the reference.
